GAME MODES

Pick your battlefield.

Each mode plays differently, but every piece matters.

Blitzkrieg

Choose a Pawn, Knight, Bishop, Rook, or Queen. Move in real time, read incoming attacks, and defend your King.

Choose a faction and arrange your army on a hex board. When the round starts, the pieces fight on their own.
