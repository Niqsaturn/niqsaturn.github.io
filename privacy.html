Privacy and account information

Last updated November 22, 2026

En-Route: Blitzkrieg is a Windows game by Niqsaturn. This notice explains the information needed to create an account, sign in and optionally send feedback.

Account details

Registration requires a username, email address and password. The account service stores a password hash, not a readable password. It protects stored email addresses using encryption and uses a separate cryptographic email identifier to check uniqueness and account recovery. Email verification is required before creating a new account.

Verification email

The account service uses a transactional-email delivery provider to send verification and recovery messages. Verification codes expire after 15 minutes. Do not share those codes, passwords or recovery information with anyone.

Game progress and diagnostics

The Windows client encrypts local saves on your device. Removing local application data may remove that progress. Portable cloud-save transfers are optional and require separate permission. Where you enable optional performance and gameplay metrics, the service may process event counts and gameplay diagnostics. Feedback you choose to send is handled separately from your local saves.

Community forum

The public En-Route forum lets players browse discussions, start topics and reply using their existing game accounts. Public posts display the account username, topic content, replies and timestamps. Forum messages are stored by the En-Route forum API, not GitHub Issues. Topics and replies are also copied to the private player-feedback review service for moderation and game improvements. Do not publish information that you want to keep private. The sign-in and posting form does not store your password or access token in browser storage.

Advertising on the website and forum

The public En-Route website and forum overview use Adsterra banner and native-banner placements for visitors who have not verified an active Royal Pass ad-free membership. These third-party ads may use cookies or related technologies to deliver, measure and personalize advertisements. A signed-in member can verify their Royal Pass on a separate ad-free page, which saves only a short-lived receipt used to suppress website and forum ad loading. Adsterra scripts are never placed in the Windows game, registration, account sign-in, payment, or forum posting pages. See Adsterra's cookie policy and its related privacy information for details. You never need to click advertisements to read the forum or register. Advertising may be blocked by browser preferences or security tools.

Purchases

The promotional website does not collect payment card information. Optional in-game purchases, when available, are handled through a hosted payment provider, subject to that provider's privacy practices and the game's billing terms. A displayed price is not a guarantee that checkout is enabled.

Access and account requests

You can use the in-game feedback mechanism to ask about data, account access, correction or deletion. These requests may require account-ownership verification. Do not include your password or account-recovery codes in feedback.

Updates

This notice may be revised as game services change. We will update the date shown above when there is a material change.
